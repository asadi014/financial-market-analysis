# Construction Materials Market Analysis — 2026

## Cement Market in the Iran Mercantile Exchange

### Overview

This case study analyzes the **cement market traded on the Iran Mercantile Exchange (IME)** during the first six months of each year from **1401 to 1405**.

The analysis focuses on changes in **supply, demand, traded volume, pricing, and market competition**, with additional comparison between cement price growth and exchange-rate movements.

The objective is to identify how market activity and price dynamics have evolved over time and to explore the relationship between market pressure and price movements.

---

## Analysis Period

**H1 1401 – H1 1405**

All comparisons are based on the first six months of each year to maintain a consistent time frame.

---

## Market Indicators

| Year | Demand / Supply | Trade / Supply | Competition |
| ---- | --------------: | -------------: | ----------: |
| 1401 |             98% |            80% |          2% |
| 1402 |            122% |            92% |          5% |
| 1403 |            123% |            89% |          4% |
| 1404 |            129% |            94% |          3% |
| 1405 |            143% |            91% |       0.46% |

### Key Observations

* The **Demand-to-Supply ratio** increased from 98% in H1 1401 to 143% in H1 1405.
* The **Trade-to-Supply ratio** remained relatively high throughout the period, reaching 91% in H1 1405.
* **Price competition** declined from 5% in 1402 to 0.46% in 1405.
* Higher demand pressure did not result in a proportional increase in price competition.

---

## Cement Price vs. Exchange Rate

| Year | Cement Price Growth | Free-Market USD Growth | Exchange Center Rate Growth |
| ---- | ------------------: | ---------------------: | --------------------------: |
| 1402 |               17.8% |                  67.1% |                           — |
| 1403 |               42.0% |                  19.8% |                       14.0% |
| 1404 |               78.3% |                  50.0% |                       61.5% |
| 1405 |               77.3% |                 101.6% |                      115.6% |

The comparison shows that the relationship between cement price growth and exchange-rate growth was **not constant over the observed period**.

In some years, cement prices grew more slowly than the USD exchange rate, while in other years they increased faster.

This indicates that exchange-rate movements alone may not fully explain changes in cement prices.

---

## 1405 Snapshot

H1 1405 presents a particularly interesting market configuration:

* **Demand / Supply:** 143%
* **Trade / Supply:** 91%
* **Competition:** 0.46%
* **Cement Price Growth:** 77.3%
* **Free-Market USD Growth:** 101.6%
* **Exchange Center Rate Growth:** 115.6%

The combination of high demand pressure, high trade conversion, low competition, and slower cement price growth than exchange-rate growth provides an important basis for deeper analysis.
