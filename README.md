# Weekly Financial Market Analysis

## Overview

This project presents a data-driven weekly analysis of the financial
market, with a focus on the Iranian stock market (TSE).

The objective is to analyze market performance, trading activity,
industry behavior, and key market indicators, and to extract
meaningful insights from the available data.

## Objectives

- Analyze weekly market performance
- Evaluate key market indicators
- Compare industry and sector performance
- Analyze trading volume and trading value
- Identify significant market trends
- Generate data-driven market insights
